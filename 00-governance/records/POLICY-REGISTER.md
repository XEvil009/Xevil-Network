1. Purpose

This register records the lifecycle, ownership, approval, ratification, and review status of X-Evil Network governance documents.

Creating, publishing, approving, or merging a document must not be represented as formal ratification unless the applicable ratification process has been completed and recorded.

2. Policy status definitions

Status| Meaning
PROPOSED| Under review; not binding
APPROVED| Accepted by the designated authority; effectiveness may be pending
RATIFIED| Formally adopted with an auditable decision record
ACTIVE| Ratified and currently effective
DEPRECATED| Superseded or no longer recommended
RETIRED| No longer in force

For each ratification, record:

- Policy identifier and version.
- Approving authority.
- Explicit approval or decision reference.
- Ratification date and effective date.
- Pull request or other auditable decision record.
- Superseded version, if applicable.
- Required transition actions.
